Grades and planning

Grade calculators produce mathematical estimates. They do not account for every institutional policy, curve, dropped assessment, minimum exam mark, or rounding rule. Official records and your institution’s rules determine actual grades.

Text and timing

Word counts vary among programs, and reading or speaking times are estimates. Review transformed text before using it. No tool provides plagiarism detection or a guarantee that work satisfies an assignment.
