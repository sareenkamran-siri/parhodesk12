About ParhoDesk

ParhoDesk is maintained by Sareen Kamran. It is a small collection of free tools for students, writers, and anyone who wants to check everyday calculations. It is intended primarily for college and university study. Its purpose is to reduce repetitive work while making the method behind each answer easier to understand.

What you can do here

Check a draft’s length, clean pasted text, plan a presentation, estimate assessment results, or convert a measurement. Each tool includes instructions, a worked example, and an explanation of its limits. The literature collection contains identified public-domain source editions, with their original notices and licenses.

How the tools are designed

Calculations and text changes happen in your browser. There is no ParhoDesk account, and the tools do not upload or save your inputs. Reloading a page clears your work, so copy results you want to keep.

Learning comes first

We show formulas and assumptions instead of treating a result as an unexplained answer. The guides are educational material, not institutional grading policies. For assessed work, your assignment instructions remain the source of truth.
