Our editorial approach

ParhoDesk content explains the specific operations its tools perform. Guides and tool explanations are prepared for this site with AI assistance and human editorial responsibility. They combine plain-language methods with worked examples. The literature texts are reproduced source editions, not original ParhoDesk writing.

Scope and accuracy

We state the formula, input assumptions, and limitations on each tool page. Examples illustrate a method; they are not predictions of a student’s official results. Institution-specific policies and specialist advice are outside our scope.

Corrections

If you find an error, contact us with the page and a reproducible example. Corrections should update both the explanation and the calculation when relevant. The date on a guide indicates its latest content revision.

Advertising and independence

This version does not load advertising scripts or paid placements. Before advertising is introduced, placements and the affected pages must be reviewed. Ads must be clearly labelled and kept separate from tool controls, print buttons, and explanations. Full-book readers are not approved advertising placements in the current site plan. Advertising must not change the formulas used by the tools.
