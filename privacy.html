Privacy policy

Effective October 1, 2026. This policy describes the current ParhoDesk website.

Text and calculator inputs

Calculator and text-tool inputs are processed locally without server storage. The semester dashboard is an exception to temporary input handling: it saves semester details, subjects, classes and deadlines in this browser’s local storage. This data does not sync across devices or get uploaded by ParhoDesk. Use Download backup to keep a copy, Import backup to restore it, and Clear dashboard to delete saved dashboard data. Clearing browser data also removes it. On a shared device, other users of the same browser profile may see it. Keep backups private. Other calculator and text-tool inputs are lost when you leave or reload their pages.

Hosting and access

The hosting platform receives ordinary requests when you open a page or asset. Technical information such as IP address, browser information, request time, and requested URL may be processed by the platform for delivery and security. If the site is accessed through a private preview, the platform may also handle authentication. The site owner does not control the platform’s retention settings.

Email

If you contact the owner, your email provider and the receiving email service process your message, email address, and any information you include. This information is used to understand and respond to the request. Do not email sensitive material that is unnecessary for your question.

Reading bookmarks and preferences

When you choose Save my place, the reader stores the book, chapter, and position in this browser’s local storage. Your chosen reading text size is also saved locally. These preferences are not sent to ParhoDesk servers and do not sync across devices. Use Clear bookmark in a book’s reader to remove its bookmark, or clear this site’s browser data to remove all reader preferences. If browser storage is unavailable, reading still works but saving does not.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

This version does not include ParhoDesk analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, or local-storage tracking. Hosting or authentication services may use their own necessary technologies. No Google AdSense code is active on this version.

If Google advertising is enabled later, this policy must be updated to identify the actual vendors and explain advertising cookies and user choices before ads are activated. Google and other vendors may use cookies based on prior visits; Google’s advertising cookies can support personalized ads across sites. Google’s Ad settings provides personalization controls. Required consent controls must also be configured for the audiences served.

Your questions

For questions about information you have emailed to the site owner, contact parhodesk@gmail.com. Describe your request without including unnecessary sensitive data. Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated effective date.
