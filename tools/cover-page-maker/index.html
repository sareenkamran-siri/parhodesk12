Assignment cover-page maker

Create a clean, printable assignment cover page.

Processed on this device. Inputs are not saved by ParhoDesk.

How to use the assignment cover-page maker

Enter an assignment title, subject, student name, and roll number. Institution, department, teacher, semester, and submission date are optional. Create the preview, then select Print / Save PDF. In the browser print dialog, choose A4 paper and turn off headers and footers if they are not needed.

A worked example

For an OOP assignment, you could enter Assignment 2 as the title, Object-Oriented Programming as the subject, and your own student details. Optional blank fields are left out rather than printed as placeholders.

Method and limitations

This is a general cover-page layout, not an official university template. Check any required formatting before submission. Long titles or institution names may use additional space, so inspect the print preview. Details stay in the browser and are not saved when you reload; keep your exported PDF.

Before you use the result

Check your input against the original source, especially after pasting or changing units. For coursework, follow the instructions and rounding rules given by your teacher. Treat the tool as a checking aid and keep your working where it is required.

Do I need an account?

No. All of the tools work without a ParhoDesk account.

Will my text or marks be uploaded?

The tool calculations run in your browser. ParhoDesk does not send these inputs to a server or retain them after you leave or reload this page.
