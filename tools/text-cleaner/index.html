WRITING TOOL

Text cleaner

Remove extra spaces and tidy pasted text.

Processed on this device. Inputs are not saved by ParhoDesk.

How to use the text cleaner

Use this for drafts copied from notes or documents. It removes repeated horizontal spaces and trims each line while preserving paragraph breaks.

A worked example

“Study a little daily” becomes “Study a little daily”. Blank lines remain so your paragraphs do not merge.

Method and limitations

Cleanup cannot fix grammar, restore missing words, or reliably reconstruct a PDF layout. Keep an original copy when spacing carries meaning, such as poetry or source code.

Before you use the result

Check your input against the original source, especially after pasting or changing units. For coursework, follow the instructions and rounding rules given by your teacher. Treat the tool as a checking aid and keep your working where it is required.

Do I need an account?

No. All of the tools work without a ParhoDesk account.

Will my text or marks be uploaded?

The tool calculations run in your browser. ParhoDesk does not send these inputs to a server or retain them after you leave or reload this page.
