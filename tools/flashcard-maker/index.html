STUDY TOOL

Flashcard maker

Turn paragraphs or question-and-answer notes into revision cards.

Processed on this device. Inputs are not saved by ParhoDesk.

How to use the flashcard maker

Choose Paragraph mode and paste normal notes, then select Create flashcards. Simple English is/are/was/were sentences become definition questions. Other sentences become missing-word questions. Choose Question: answer pairs to keep full control over the wording. Flip cards, edit either side, shuffle, or filter by revision status.

A worked example

RAM is temporary memory used by a computer. becomes a question asking what RAM is, with temporary memory used by a computer as the answer. The CPU processes instructions. becomes a missing-word question, with a word from that sentence as its answer.

Method and limitations

This free tool uses local text rules, not AI. It does not understand a chapter, identify the most important facts, resolve pronouns, or verify correctness. English works best for definition questions; other languages use missing-word prompts. Check every generated card. Very short sentences are skipped and exact duplicate sentences are ignored. Up to 200 cards and 100,000 characters are accepted. Cards, edits, and progress are lost on reload or navigation, so keep your own notes.

Before you use the result

Check your input against the original source, especially after pasting or changing units. For coursework, follow the instructions and rounding rules given by your teacher. Treat the tool as a checking aid and keep your working where it is required.

Do I need an account?

No. All of the tools work without a ParhoDesk account.

Will my text or marks be uploaded?

The tool calculations run in your browser. ParhoDesk does not send these inputs to a server or retain them after you leave or reload this page.
