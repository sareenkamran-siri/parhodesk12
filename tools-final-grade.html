Final exam calculator

Find the exam score needed for your target grade.

Processed on this device. Inputs are not saved by ParhoDesk.

How to use the final exam calculator

Enter your average before the final, your target overall grade, and the final exam weight. The current average should exclude the final exam.

A worked example

With an 80% current average, an 85% target, and a final worth 40%, you need 92.5%: (85 − 80 × 0.60) ÷ 0.40.

Method and limitations

A required score above 100% is unattainable under the ordinary scoring assumptions. A negative score means the target is already secured if a zero on the final is allowed. Minimum exam-pass rules are not included.

Before you use the result

Check your input against the original source, especially after pasting or changing units. For coursework, follow the instructions and rounding rules given by your teacher. Treat the tool as a checking aid and keep your working where it is required.

Do I need an account?

No. All of the tools work without a ParhoDesk account.

Will my text or marks be uploaded?

The tool calculations run in your browser. ParhoDesk does not send these inputs to a server or retain them after you leave or reload this page.
